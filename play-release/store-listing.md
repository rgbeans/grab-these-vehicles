# Google Play listing draft

**App name:** grab these vehicles

**Short description:** Random Simeon vehicle requests with a GTA chime and tap-to-copy.

**Full description:**

Simeon has another job for you.

Get random “Grab these vehicles” notifications inspired by Simeon's requests in GTA Online. Set your preferred minimum and maximum wait, then let Android deliver the next request. The default is a random wait of 30–60 minutes.

- 16 English vehicle-request lists from the original and updated game eras.
- Bundled GTA notification chime, with sound and vibration controlled by Android.
- Tap a notification to copy the complete message.
- Tap the latest-request card in the app to copy the message shown there.
- Adjustable intervals, pause/resume, and an immediate test notification.
- Reboot restoration and a background recovery check.
- Works offline, with no account, ads, analytics, or internet permission.

Open the app once to allow notifications. For dependable background use, follow the app's timing, unused-app, and battery-setting guidance. Android can delay background work, and a force-stopped app cannot run until you open it again.

This is an unofficial fan app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. Third-party game content belongs to its respective rights holders.
