Privacy policy · Updated October 5, 2026

Draft: add the publisher’s contact details before hosting or submitting this policy.

Local data

The app stores your interval settings, enabled state, next alarm deadlines, latest request, and shuffled message rotation on your device. It does not collect or send personal data.

No tracking

The app has no internet permission, ads, analytics, sign-in, billing, or tracking SDKs.

Notifications and clipboard

Android handles notifications. Tapping a notification or the latest-request card copies that message to the system clipboard. You choose whether to paste it into another app. The app never reads your existing clipboard.

Notification sound

A public, read-only content provider makes the bundled notification sound readable to Android. It exposes only that audio clip, not saved messages, settings, or personal files.

Optional native calls

Calls use your existing Phone app. Contacts access creates and manages only a separate on-device GTA Callers account with fictional numbers, character pictures, and a custom ringtone. It does not sign in or sync to a server. The app never records microphone audio or places outgoing calls. Imported recordings stay in app storage. Simulated calls may appear in Phone history, which is managed separately by your Phone app.

Deletion and device services

Clear app storage to remove settings and imported recordings. Use Remove GTA caller contacts to delete its local caller account and contacts; clearing app storage alone does not remove contacts. Android device backups, device transfers, notification settings, and clipboard behavior are controlled by your phone.
